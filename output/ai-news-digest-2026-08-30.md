## 2. Sony Music and Warner Chappell are suing Anthropic

**Source:** The Verge AI | **Published:** 2026-08-29 18:19 UTC

Sony Music and Warner Chappell have sued Anthropic in the US District Court for the Northern District of California over "tens of thousands" of copyrighted works, seeking up to $150,000 per work plus $25,000 for each instance of stripped copyright management information — potentially several billion dollars. The complaint names co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann as individual defendants, alleging Mann torrented over five million pirated books and that employees pulled two million more from Pirate Library Mirror, while Anthropic also scraped lyrics from licensed services like MusixMatch and LyricFind. It follows Anthropic's recent $1.5 billion settlement with book publishers and existing suits from Universal Music Group, Concord, ABKCO, BMG, and Round Hill Music.

It also advises companies to hire more engineers, not fewer, since faster time-to-market is a competitive advantage, and offers a checklist of culture-assessment questions covering ownership, decision autonomy, trust, and blameless learning from failure.

## 6. Debian votes to allow "responsible use of generative AI"

**Source:** Hacker News AI | **Published:** 2026-08-29 14:02 UTC

Debian has concluded its general resolution vote on large language models, with option 5, "Responsible Use of Generative AI," winning. The resolution takes a neutral stance, neither endorsing nor prohibiting generative AI tools in Debian development, packaging, or documentation, while acknowledging they can boost contributor productivity when used responsibly. All contributions must still meet the same standards of quality, correctness, maintainability, and legal compliance, and contributors remain fully responsible for reviewing, testing, and understanding AI-assisted output they submit.
