## 2. OpenAI seeks to one-up Anthropic with new customer privacy protections

**Source:** TechCrunch AI | **Published:** 2026-08-19 22:10 UTC

OpenAI is previewing "Private Safety Processing" with select customers, an automated system that monitors for misuse across multiple conversation sessions without retaining any customer data. The tool extends existing Zero Data Retention practices by using agents to flag long-horizon abuse patterns — such as a bad actor splitting malware requests across sessions — sending OpenAI only a "narrowly defined signal" rather than exposing conversations to human review.

The move is a direct swipe at Anthropic, whose July policy retains user data for 30 days on "covered models" and permits limited human review through logged, controlled access — a practice that has unsettled enterprises handling sensitive data.
