## 3. Amazon, which started off selling books, is destroying rare texts to train AI

**Source:** TechCrunch AI | **Published:** 2026-08-17 16:38 UTC

## 8. Anthropic explains how Claude's invisible text watermarks will work

**Source:** The Verge AI | **Published:** 2026-08-17 10:57 UTC

Anthropic announced Friday that it will watermark Claude's text output using "a version of" Google DeepMind's open-source SynthID-Text system, which embeds detectable patterns by using a cryptographic key to guide the model's low-stakes word choices rather than a standard random number generator. The measure, along with C2PA metadata support for Claude-processed images, is meant to satisfy the EU AI Act's requirement that synthetic content carry machine-readable markers; Anthropic says it won't raise costs or affect output quality. Google's Gemini has used SynthID-Text since 2024, while OpenAI has yet to detail text watermarking plans despite facing the same obligations.
