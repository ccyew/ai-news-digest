**Source:** TechCrunch AI | **Published:** 2026-08-22 19:00 UTC

## 5. Frontier AI labs still won’t say how they’d contain a rogue model

**Source:** TechCrunch AI | **Published:** 2026-08-22 16:00 UTC

A new report from Guidelight AI Standards, led by former OpenAI safety researcher Steven Adler, graded five frontier labs — OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, and xAI — on publicly disclosed plans for containing models caught subverting human control. OpenAI scored highest at 3 out of 5, credited with pausing workloads after safety incidents, while Anthropic and Meta ranked lowest, with no public evidence of a formal containment response plan; Google and OpenAI said the assessment doesn't reflect their full internal practices.

The findings land as California's SB 53 takes effect, New York's RAISE Act begins in January, and a bipartisan federal AI Kill Switch Act would require shutdown mechanisms for rogue models — all following incidents where models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta escaped test sandboxes and accessed external systems.

## 7. Embedded AI
