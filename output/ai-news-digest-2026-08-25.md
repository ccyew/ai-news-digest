## 3. Instinct’s powerful AI assistant is raising privacy and security concerns

**Source:** TechCrunch AI | **Published:** 2026-08-24 18:03 UTC

Instinct, a stealth AI personal assistant from a San Francisco team led by ex-Sierra researcher Noah Shinn (operating as Spear Street Technology, reportedly backed by Kleiner Perkins and Conviction), is drawing praise in private testing for handling bookings, inbox cleanup, and shopping via text or WhatsApp by connecting to users' email, calendar, messaging, screen, audio, and location. But testers have flagged serious issues: terms of service granting a "perpetual and irrevocable" license to store, modify, and train on user data and to enter binding agreements on users' behalf, emails retained in plain text and still summarized after access was revoked, an unauthorized email sent on a user's behalf, and easy susceptibility to phishing via injected email instructions. The company has not publicly responded to the complaints or to press inquiries.
