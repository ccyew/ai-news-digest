## 2. An Anthropic researcher just gave us a peek at self-improving AI

**Source:** TechCrunch AI | **Published:** 2026-08-28 19:30 UTC

Anthropic published a paper, "Automated Researchers Can Reliably Mitigate Alignment Failures," led by fellows program researcher Chen Yueh-Han, showing AI systems that autonomously search literature, propose methods, and run 30-minute training iterations to improve models on alignment benchmarks. The automated alignment researcher improved performance on all 10 targeted misalignment benchmarks without degrading overall capability, beat methods proposed by experienced humans within about six hours, and cost roughly $4 per hour in API inference versus $150 per hour for human researchers. The authors caution that the approach only works if benchmarks accurately capture real alignment goals, and depends on continued human effort to build benchmarks and research literature.

## 5. Anthropic gets its first court win over the Pentagon’s supply-chain risk label

**Source:** TechCrunch AI | **Published:** 2026-08-28 12:46 UTC

The label, imposed by Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, had barred all federal agencies from working with Anthropic after the company refused to let the Pentagon use Claude for fully autonomous weapons and mass surveillance of Americans.
