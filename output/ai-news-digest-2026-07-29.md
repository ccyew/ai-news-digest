## 24. If AI Writes All the Code, What Do the Programmers Do?

**Source:** Hacker News AI | **Published:** 2026-07-28 08:17 UTC

A GPU kernel engineer describes a workflow that has flipped from ~90% human-written to ~90% AI-written code in eight months, walking through an optimization of a matrix-multiply kernel using Nvidia's Cluster Launch Control. The AI located the relevant code, diagnosed why his initial fix deadlocked, proposed reusing an unused shared-memory slot to track CLC state, wrote benchmark scripts, and — via a second reviewing model and a review bot — flagged edge cases around clobbered state and CLC work ordering.

His verdict: the initial implementation took under a day instead of two or three, but total time only dropped from about five days to four, since benchmarking, review, and comprehension dominate; the code was more thoroughly explored and more robust, though his own understanding of it is shallower than if he'd written it himself.
