## 10. Apple could help you prove your iPhone photos aren’t deepfakes

**Source:** The Verge AI | **Published:** 2026-08-11 16:19 UTC

Code found in the iOS 27 beta 5 points to an unreleased "Apple Reference Image" feature that would embed provenance metadata — including sensor signatures, capture timestamps, and hardware identifiers — into photos taken with a new "Reference" mode in the iPhone Camera app, letting users prove an image wasn't AI-generated. Verification would be off by default and manually triggered by tapping a Reference badge, which sends the raw image and metadata to Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers for authentication and returns a uniquely ID'd version; Apple says it doesn't access the raw photo but can revoke authentication tied to compromised sensors. The approach resembles the C2PA Content Credentials standard already adopted by Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Leica, and Google's Pixel 10 — a standard Apple has so far declined to support.
