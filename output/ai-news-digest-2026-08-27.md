## 14. WebMCP: Teaching Your Website to Talk to AI Agents

**Source:** Hacker News AI | **Published:** 2026-08-26 15:02 UTC

WebMCP, a proposed browser standard from Google and Microsoft under the W3C Web Machine Learning Community Group, lets websites register structured, JSON Schema-defined tools (via `document.modelContext.registerTool`) that AI agents can discover and call directly, replacing brittle DOM scraping with a stable declared interface. Tools execute the site's existing JavaScript inside the user's open, logged-in tab, and Chrome exposes registered tools in a DevTools WebMCP panel. It's available as a Chrome origin trial from Chrome 149 (or via the `chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing` flag), with Angular offering experimental support and ChatGPT already able to drive WebMCP sites — though the spec remains an early Community Group draft subject to change, as shown by the recent shift from `navigator.modelContext` to `document.modelContext`.
