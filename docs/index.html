The AI Productivity Illusion

Hacker News AI · 2026-07-25 13:43 UTC

Open Markets Institute fellow Matt Scherer argues that generative AI's apparent productivity gains are illusory, distinguishing colloquial productivity (more output, faster) from economic total factor productivity, which weighs the value of outputs against inputs. He contends that AI's error-proneness—fabricated case law, deleted code, incorrect diagnoses—devalues output while inflating quality-control costs, and points to a $1.5 trillion AI infrastructure build-out that must be recouped. Citing Financial Times data showing app releases nearly doubling while apps with meaningful usage decline, he suggests AI produces more stuff without new economic value, and that harmful use cases like AI-enabled cyberattacks and "workslop" may actively destroy productivity.
