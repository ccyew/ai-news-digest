Anti-AI fonts are useless and harmful

Hacker News AI · 2026-08-20 15:06 UTC

An opinion piece argues that "anti-AI" fonts like Decoy, Shield, and Ghost — which scramble or obfuscate text to thwart scrapers — are counterproductive, because screen readers and other accessibility tools parse the same scrambled output, locking out disabled readers. The author contends any accessible workaround would require machine-readable metadata and thus centralized identity verification, and that public demos effectively serve as benchmarks that AI firms have already learned to defeat. The broader warning: an obfuscation arms race pushes the web toward copy-protection and paywalls, undermining open access, and publishers should instead plan around the assumption that any publicly visible information will be machine-readable.
