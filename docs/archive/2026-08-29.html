TechCrunch AI · 2026-08-28 19:30 UTC

Anthropic published a paper, "Automated Researchers Can Reliably Mitigate Alignment Failures," led by fellows program researcher Chen Yueh-Han, showing AI systems that autonomously search literature, propose methods, and run 30-minute training iterations to improve models on alignment benchmarks.
