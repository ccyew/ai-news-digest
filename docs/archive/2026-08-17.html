The AI Credit Resale Economy

Hacker News AI · 2026-08-16 14:44 UTC

A security researcher documents a growing gray market where "token brokers" buy unused startup cloud and inference credits — often from accelerator programs like YC Startup School — and resell them at 30–80% off list, either through marketplaces like AI Credits and AICreditMart or via proxy routers such as CheapCredits, Tokvana and Neokens that pitch discounts as "bulk pricing." Direct outreach to one broker turned up an offer of $100,000 per day in spend, routed through a proxy that forwards requests using a pool of provider keys rather than sharing keys directly. AI Coding Without the Vibes

Hacker News AI · 2026-08-16 10:31 UTC

Blog essay argues that instead of "vibe-coding," where developers delegate code generation to AI and rarely review the output, students and researchers should write code by hand and use AI as a reviewer/critic — summarized as "hand-written, AI reviewed." The author, an assistant professor, says the doing/checking split matters because humans can't reliably audit large volumes of machine-generated code, and over-reliance erodes the ability to judge one's own understanding. He calls this approach "craft coding" (illustrated via an analogy to home, commercial, and craft bakers) and argues it's especially important for scientific code, where correctness underpins a paper's validity; he notes Claude has found serious bugs in every snippet of his own code he's submitted for review.
