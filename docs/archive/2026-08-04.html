Europe’s AI labeling and transparency rules are now in effect

The Verge AI · 2026-08-03 17:38 UTC

The EU AI Act's transparency obligations took effect on August 2nd, requiring AI providers to disclose when users are interacting with AI and embed machine-readable markers in synthetic audio, image, video, and text, while deployers must label realistic AI-generated deepfake content. The European Commission also released optional standardized disclosure icons — similar to those already used by TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook — though the underlying labeling requirements themselves are mandatory. Non-compliance can trigger fines of up to €15 million or 3 percent of global annual turnover, with systems launched before August 2nd given a grace period until December 2nd.
